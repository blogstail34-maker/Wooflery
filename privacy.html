The important bits

Your privacy matters.

Wooflery stores account information, dog profiles, uploaded photos, likes, comments and moderation records in its local database to provide the community features.

What you share

Only upload images you have the right to share. Public dog profiles, their photos and comments are visible to visitors. Never publish private or sensitive personal information.

Account security

Passwords are hashed before storage. Sessions use HTTP-only cookies. For production deployment, configure a strong session secret, HTTPS, backups, and appropriate retention and privacy practices.

Questions about this sample policy? Use the contact page. This starter policy should be reviewed and adapted to the operator and laws that apply before public launch.
